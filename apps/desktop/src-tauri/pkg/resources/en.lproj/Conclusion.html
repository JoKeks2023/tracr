tracr is installed

Open it from Launchpad or Spotlight.

Next steps:

  • Open tracr and approve the provider folders you want it to read. Nothing is read before that.
  • In Terminal: tracr import, then tracr day.

To remove it again: delete /Applications/tracr.app and /usr/local/bin/tracr. Your ledger stays in ~/Library/Application Support/tracr until you delete it.

## 3. How parallel sessions are represented

- **Agent timelines** are one per session, and per identified actor. They overlap freely and every session is logged, stored and displayed individually (AC-13, RP-09). They are never collapsed into one "active session".
- **The human timeline** is separate and reconciled. Each instant is counted at most once (AC-02).
- **A human interval** carries `engagedContexts` and `concurrentAgentContexts`. It may be attributed **joint** (unsplit, counted once). tracr invents no split (AC-14, AT-06) ([concepts §6](../../concepts.md#6-concurrent-sessions-and-contexts), [activity-model §9](../../activity-model.md#9-concurrent-agent-sessions-and-the-human-timeline)).
- **Report identity (RP-07).** The counted human total is per-target exclusive + joint + counted work without a resolved target. Per-target figures never exceed it.
- **Walkthrough C.** Three overlapping sessions across two providers: 134 agent minutes in 80 wall-clock minutes. 55 human minutes counted after review, of which 3 are joint. Not 58. An unattended session gets 0 human minutes, but all 59 agent minutes and its usage are logged ([walkthrough C](../../walkthroughs.md#c-parallel-sessions), [D](../../walkthroughs.md#d-mixed-providers)).

## 4. How A→B→A no longer counts unknown time

| Outcome | A | B | Counted | Unknown | Non-work |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Unresolved (default) | 57 | 30 | 87 | 20, visible | 0 |
| Break | 57 | 30 | 87 | 0 | 20 |
| User-confirmed work | 64 | 43 | 107 | 0 | 0 |
| Partial | 64 | 30 | 94 | 0 | 13 |

The 87 minutes are inferred and provisional, and they depend on the assumed continuation rule. 107 is reached only through two user assertions. Without them, 107 is the evidence span and serves only as an upper bound. The interleaved variant counts 0 inferred minutes, with 25 unknown. The research's 35-versus-25 comparison demonstrates overcounting. It does not validate 25 human minutes.

## 6. Deviations and additions to note

- **New document.** `docs/walkthroughs.md` was added. All other documents keep their structure, but several sections were renumbered and cross-references were updated.
- **New spec vocabulary** that the review did not name explicitly:
  - joint attribution (an unsplit multi-context interval);
  - `engagedContexts` / `concurrentAgentContexts`;
  - completeness values `unverified` / `verified` / `recovered` / `gap`;
  - actor values `known` / `main` / `unknown`;
  - record lifecycle states.
- **Why those terms were added.** Each one represents a requested requirement without choosing architecture.
- **Declared MVP defaults.** They are labeled as such, not as decisions of open questions:
  - window policies only suggest, and unknown stays unknown until the user acts;
  - payload is `never_stored`;
  - a trimmed-off range becomes `non_work`.
- **RP-07 formula.** It also lists counted work whose attribution is ambiguous or conflicting, because otherwise its terms would not sum to the counted total.
- **Illustrative data.** Walkthrough values are illustrative. Walkthrough G's split into main-agent and unknown-actor usage rests on a stated assumption, because the research does not establish main-agent identification for Codex usage records.
